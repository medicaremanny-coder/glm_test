# Review Before Posting - Questions For Manny

Created: 2026-07-03

Use this before any TikTok, Reel, Short, or Facebook post goes public.

## Simple Rule

If Manny cannot explain the post out loud in plain Spanish, do not publish it yet.

## Questions To Ask Yourself

1. What is the one question this post answers?
2. Can I explain it without using insurance jargon?
3. Would a 65-year-old understand this in one listen?
4. Am I naming any plan, carrier, premium, benefit, product count, or county count?
5. Am I accidentally making it sound like I am Medicare or the government?
6. Is the call to action simple: comment GUIA, visit medicaremanny.com, or schedule?
7. Does the visual match the message?

## Questions To Ask A Client Or Viewer

Use these to learn better wording:

1. When you hear this, what part is confusing?
2. What word would you use instead?
3. Would you say this sounds like an ad or like advice?
4. What would you ask me next after watching this?
5. Does this make you want a checklist, a call, or more explanation?

## Words To Avoid Or Translate

Avoid:

- acreditable
- coordinación de beneficios
- penalidad tardía
- cobertura grupal primaria / secundaria
- red del plan
- formulario
- periodo de elección

Use instead:

- "cobertura válida para Medicare"
- "cómo trabaja su seguro del trabajo con Medicare"
- "problemas o multas por fechas"
- "cuál seguro paga primero"
- "si su doctor está dentro del plan"
- "lista de medicinas"
- "fechas para cambiar o escoger"

## Plain Spanish Examples

Technical:

"Pregunte si su cobertura es acreditable."

Plain:

"Pregunte si su seguro del trabajo cuenta como cobertura válida para Medicare."

Even plainer:

"Pregunte si su seguro del trabajo es suficiente para no meterse en problemas con Medicare después."

Technical:

"Coordina con Medicare."

Plain:

"Pregunte cuál seguro paga primero: Medicare o el seguro del trabajo."

Technical:

"AEP."

Plain:

"La temporada para revisar o cambiar planes empieza el 15 de octubre."

## Approval Checklist

- [ ] Manny understands every line.
- [ ] Manny can explain it in Spanish without reading.
- [ ] No Medicare card image.
- [ ] No carrier logo.
- [ ] No plan-specific claim.
- [ ] Disclaimer is visible or in caption.
- [ ] CTA is simple.
